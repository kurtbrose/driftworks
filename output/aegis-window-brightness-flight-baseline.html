Standard controllers use the fixed layout above. Full right-stick deflection changes throttle from 0% to 100% in two seconds; smaller movements adjust it more slowly. Neutral holds the setting. Move a controller stick to hide the mouse guide; move the captured mouse to bring it back. Thrustmaster and generic flight sticks default to pull-back pitch, X-axis roll, twist-axis yaw, and absolute throttle. Trigger fires; buttons 1/2/7 boost, switch bank, and pause (zero-based). Axis numbering varies by browser and device: use the live axis display below to identify your twist and throttle, then change bindings or invert as needed.
