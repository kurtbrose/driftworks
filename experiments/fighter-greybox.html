DRIFTWORKS / FIGHTER STUDY 02

Barracuda.

Compare variants A-E

A compact hatch-friendly profile: two crew sit within the fuselage centerline, with a wraparound canopy and three integrated engines.

Wraparound roof, side and rear-quarter glazing surrounds the two seated crew. A sloping windscreen and low nose panes improve the forward and downward view. A low center-console housing between the crew encloses the raised cannon, whose short muzzle emerges near the lower engine axis. The cockpit rear meets the fuselage at full height. Three enlarged engines nearly touch in an equilateral rear cross section: two above, with the lower engine staggered forward, inside the compact hatch profile.

Side, front and top presets plus an independent orbit view. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom; Reset views restores the comparison. The pale Spitfire is 12 m long.
