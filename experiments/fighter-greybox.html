DRIFTWORKS / FIGHTER STUDY 02

Barracuda.

A compact hatch-friendly profile: two crew sit within the fuselage centerline, with a wraparound canopy and integrated twin engines.

Wraparound roof, side and rear-quarter glazing surrounds the two seated crew. A sloping windscreen and low nose panes improve the forward and downward view. A slim centerline gun emerges directly from the lower nose. The cockpit rear meets the fuselage at full height. Twin engines blend into the aft fuselage with flush rear exhausts.

Side, front and top presets plus an independent orbit view. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom; Reset views restores the comparison. The pale Spitfire is 12 m long.

Provisional volume study. Dimensions are for visual comparison.
