Into the light

A bright star, a sphere standing in for the asteroid, and a camera adapting between them. Drag to orbit. Shift-drag to look around. Wheel to approach.

Seven cached linear HDR maps + finite-source lighting lookup. No ambient fill or point-source glare in this mode. The small sphere casts onto the large sphere. Toggle shadows to compare cached lighting alone. Replay by scrubbing to zero.

What to look for

At the limb, only the exposed part of the star contributes glare. The rock stays opaque. Turning toward the light lowers exposure; looking away lets shadow detail recover. The asteroid is lit from the same fixed sky direction as the star.

Floating-point light values are tone-mapped to the display. Bloom and camera adaptation are illustrative, not a calibrated camera model. No lens-ghost chain. The star is at infinity; the asteroid has depth.
