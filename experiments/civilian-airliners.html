Civilian airliners

Two related passenger transports: porcelain alloy, oxide red livery, warm cabin glazing, swept shoulders and integrated drives. Blender geometry studies at two distinct scales.

Revised flight decks: forward-facing raked windshields, corner glazing, low consoles and human-scale pilot seating. Sampled sightlines from both seats cover 30° left/right and 10° down to 15° up.

Standing Quaternius astronauts are 1.80 m tall in both scenes. Three crew stand beside each ship; the closer apron views make the size difference easier to judge.
