# Economy and colony work: design direction

These are intended gameplay rules, not implemented mechanics. The current
asteroid material artwork is an illustrative prototype; mining still uses ore.

| Material | Intended use | Visual language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ice | Primarily fuel: directly for the mothership; electrolyzed into hydrogen and oxygen for other craft | Blue-gray, pale angular patches, fine fractures |
| Volatiles | Feedstock for biomass, including carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus | Muted brown/charcoal with mottled deposits |
| Metals | Construction | Slate gray with dull silver or warm metallic inclusions |
| Silicates | Other construction materials | Warm gray, sparse craters and facets |

Mining targets should determine the mix of these four materials. Material is
effectively unlimited at mission scale; available onboard storage limits how
much can be collected. Future simulation work should make the visible deposits
and extraction mix agree, rather than treating decorative colors as resources.

The mothership is a general contractor and troubleshooter for fixed colonies.
Its jobs include preparing a site (excavating caverns, separating minerals,
installing facilities), transporting settlers, evacuating people and cleaning up
after disasters, building expansions, and providing security. Site preparation
and passenger transport may be parts of the same mission.
