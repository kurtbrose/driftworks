Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opaque albedo texture atlas for the existing armor of a kilometer-long science-fiction capital ship in a real-time game.
Primary request: make a richly authored complete painted-metal armor material, with the convincing industrial surface quality of a detailed spaceship hangar. This is the entire surface material, not a decal sheet or isolated decals.
Composition: square image divided precisely into four equal square texture tiles in a 2 by 2 grid, no gaps. Each quadrant is a complete flat orthographic armor surface, filling its square edge to edge. Top left: broad horizontal hull armor plates. Top right: broad roof plates. Bottom left: alternate hull plates. Bottom right: alternate roof plates. Each quadrant has a quiet narrow 3 percent perimeter of the same paint, with all meaningful details inset beyond that perimeter, for texture-atlas padding.
Materials: pale neutral warm gray ceramic-painted steel that will be tinted beige by the game. Large clean plate fields with substantial subtle paint tonal variation, convincing shallow recessed seams, small countersunk fasteners, a few flush access covers and recessed latches, occasional tiny neutral maintenance stencils that are not readable words. Restrained dark seam dirt, scuffing at service edges, subtle rubbed paint exposing gray steel. Broad readable industrial construction, believable functional assembly and scale. Fine workmanship with relatively well-maintained surfaces. Each quadrant has 4 to 6 major plates and a different arrangement. Keep around 75 percent quiet painted surface.
Lighting: flat neutral diffuse albedo, evenly lit, no cast shadows, no scene lighting gradient, no perspective, no directional reflections; only delicate local crevice shading to suggest shallow recesses.
Avoid: glowing lights, windows, pipes, deep machinery, hazard stripes, logos, legible text, thick black borders, heavy rust, large paint chips, dense tiny greebles, shiny highlights, extruded bevel illusion, fake perspective. Opaque square texture image only.
