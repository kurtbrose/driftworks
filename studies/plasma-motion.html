Driftworks / visual experiments

Plasma, without the lance

Six synchronized views of identical shots. Compare the motion, especially at 30 FPS and during a camera sweep. Every orb stays sharp; only the projectile receives extra exposures.

This is a screen-space motion study, not a change to weapon physics or the flight renderer. Speeds are illustrative metres/second; orb radius is deliberately held in CSS pixels to isolate motion readability. Samples cover the last rendered interval × exposure, including camera movement. No samples predict future positions. At extreme displacement, capped samples will still strobe.
