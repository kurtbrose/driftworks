Dark charcoal structural steel finish, generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.
The original generated PNG is preserved unchanged. See prompts.txt for the exact prompt.
UVs are mapped per source mesh on every dominant face, including narrow pieces
and end caps. The 283 selected meshes (6000 triangles) are consolidated into one
static textured batch. Original positions, normals, winding, poses and gameplay
geometry are retained. Small dark vents and service belts are excluded.
Finish tint: [0.32, 0.35, 0.37], deliberately dark to retain the existing palette.
The shield envelope includes these redistributed structural triangles and retains
its previous geometry exactly.
